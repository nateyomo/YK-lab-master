Main Conclusions

Fascia

Thinking about fascia as a passive tissue is an oversimplification.

There are articles against manual therapy for fascia claiming that it is impossible to significantly change fascia through manual therapy

We should be considering fascia within the context of inflammation in the environment

Multi-system consideration

  • Regardless of treatment philosophy/school, considering venous, nervous, arterial, and visceral structures along with musculoskeletal structures will only give you a clearer picture of your patient
